A · Bayt Safar بيت صفار

One level around a court, closed to the street.

Bayt Safar is a court house in the Wadi Safar manner: thick sand-rendered walls, one oak door, and rooms that take their light from a court open to the sky.

The living room rises above the rest to catch the late sun. Dining opens east to the pool. Everything on this page is the same 3D model you just watched come off the sheet.

Two floors and a roof, on a small plot.

Bayt Mustaqil stands on a 9 × 9 m footprint. Living and kitchen below, the bedroom above, and the roof as a third room under a pergola.

A terrace and a plunge pool sit beside it, reached through the wide opening in the east wall.
