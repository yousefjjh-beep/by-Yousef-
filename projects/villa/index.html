Villa Walkthrough

Basement, ground and first floor, built to scale from the plans (49 × 47 m plot).

Walk with WASD or the arrow keys, hold Shift to run. Drag to look around; double-click to lock the mouse. On a phone, drag the left half to walk and the right half to look. P or a floor’s “plan” button shows the top-down plan. Pick a room to jump there; stairs connect the floors.
