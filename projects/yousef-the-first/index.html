Yousef The First is a design study for a single-storey courtyard house: a gallery lit from a light court, a living room six metres tall, one long room for cooking and eating, a fourteen metre pool, and a master wing that ends in a walled spa. Every surface you walked past is drawn here at 1:200 before anything is built.
